OUR STORY

A photo keeps what we saw. A bottle of scent keeps how a place made us feel.

Travel today easily turns into a checklist of places to photograph. We take hundreds of pictures, yet once we are home very few of them still move us. And almost nothing keeps the feeling of that misty morning, or the smell of timber inside an old house.

MEMORA started from a simple question: if every destination had a scent, and every trip had a film about the person who took it, would the memory stay longer?

HOW WE WORK

Three layers of a memorable journey

Every MEMORA tour is built in exactly three steps: discover something truly local, create a scent with your own hands, and keep all of it in a film.
