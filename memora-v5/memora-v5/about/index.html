MEMORY + AURA

Every trip leaves behind a feeling of its own

MEMORA joins Memory and Aura — what we remember and what we feel. We believe the most memorable part of a trip isn't where you go, but how that place makes you feel.

So we built a different kind of travel company: small groups, local guides, a photographer who travels with you and a perfumer waiting at the end of the road.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Four principles we never compromise

These decide how we choose destinations, who we travel with and which ingredients we use.

♧

Local people at the centre

35% of every tour's revenue is paid directly to guides, artisans and host families. They are the storytellers, not the people standing backstage.

♧

Responsible travel

We keep groups small, choose locally and sustainably harvested ingredients, and avoid peak hours that disturb the community.

◉

Memories are never staged

Your photographer travels like a friend rather than a director. The best photos are the moments you never knew were being captured.

♜

Scent is a language

Every fragrance is built from real ingredients of that region, with real artisans. There is no ready-made formula that fits everywhere.

OUR JOURNEY

From one question to eight journeys

We didn't start with a business plan. We started with a small workshop in Hoi An.

2025

An idea that began with a question

Can a photo and a video keep the feeling of a place? We tested our first perfume workshop in Hoi An with six guests.

2026

MEMORA officially launches

Eight journeys across eight provinces, each with its own scent and its own process for a personalised short film.

Next

Growing with our community

Our goal is 20 journeys in Vietnam and partnerships with more than 100 local artisans, while keeping the 35% revenue share.
